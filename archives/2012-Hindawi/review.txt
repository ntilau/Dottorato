to Author:

The paper presents and improved accuracy in DOA estimation with a modified ESPRIT  algorithm by using the Cholesky factorization of the autocorrelation matrices (positive definite as they represent active power), instead of the classic eigenvalue decomposition, with a so-called "CDBA" method.

Some major remarks are to be stated:
- the chosen array geometry, with equal spacing between element and between array 
doublets implies that the number of effective array elements is of M+1 instead of 2M. 
Author should explain what is actually done. What appens if the doublets relative 
distance is different from d?
- a validation of ESPRIT implementation (and which version?) is missing and timing comparisons between the 2 techniques should be presented. To assess the Cholesky decomposition DOA estimation enhancements, Author should add more test cases compairing timings or mathematicaly demonstrate the increase in accuracy.
- it is not clear how the references come into play in the paper. Author should also specify where the ESPRIT algorithm specifications can be retrieved.
